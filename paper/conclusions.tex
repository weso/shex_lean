\section{Conclusions and future work} \label{sec:conclusions}

Using Lean, it is possible to automatically verify the different 
lemmas that relate the semantics of the ShEx language variants. 
The resulting repository can be used as a first approach to explore 
  more design decisions about the ShEx language.
Future work includes a more extensive exploration of the 
 design space for RDF validation languages including extending ShEx 
 for RDF 1.2 features or other language features~\cite{L24}.

The Lean development is available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/shex_lean-B1A5/} and it contains:
\begin{itemize}
\item the 2017 semantics and its proofs,
\item the inheritance extension and its proofs
\item all the examples from both papers associated with their corresponding numbers in the paper
\item an executable main with two command-line options: \lean{list} to show all 
 the propositions and examples that can be checked, and \lean{run} to check a specific one 
 by its name.
\end{itemize}
